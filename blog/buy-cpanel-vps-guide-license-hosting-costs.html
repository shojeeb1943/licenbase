Buy cPanel VPS: Guide to VPS, cPanel Licenses & Hosting Costs

By LicenBase Team Published October 6, 2026 4 min read

Setting up a virtual private server (VPS) with cPanel & WHM is the industry-standard foundation for agencies, developers, and hosting entrepreneurs. However, navigating the true cost of server infrastructure, software licensing tiers, and essential add-ons can be confusing. Many first-time buyers overpay by choosing expensive bundled managed hosting packages when an unbundled cloud setup delivers identical performance at a fraction of the price. This complete guide breaks down hardware requirements, compares bundled vs unbundled hosting costs, and shows you how to build an enterprise-ready cPanel VPS while keeping total monthly expenses remarkably low.

Recommended VPS Sizing for cPanel Workloads

To ensure responsive website delivery and prevent memory pressure, choose your cloud instance specifications based on your anticipated website count and database complexity:

  • Starter VPS (1 to 5 Websites): 2 GB to 4 GB RAM, 2 vCPU cores, 40 GB NVMe SSD. Perfect for agency staging, freelancer portfolios, and low-traffic business sites.
  • Production Reseller VPS (10 to 30 Websites): 4 GB to 8 GB RAM, 4 vCPU cores, 100 GB NVMe SSD. Supports dynamic WordPress sites with moderate traffic, scheduled backups, and email routing.
  • High-Density Hosting VPS (50+ Websites): 16 GB+ RAM, 6 to 8 vCPU cores, 200 GB+ NVMe SSD. Paired with LiteSpeed Web Server and CloudLinux OS for maximum multi-tenant isolation.

Step-by-Step Setup: From Fresh VPS to Active WHM

Deploying your own unbundled cPanel VPS requires just four quick steps:

  1. Deploy a fresh minimal installation of AlmaLinux 9 on your chosen cloud provider (Hetzner Cloud, DigitalOcean Droplet, Linode, or Vultr).
  2. Connect via SSH as root and set your Fully Qualified Domain Name (FQDN) hostname: 
    hostnamectl set-hostname vps1.yourdomain.com
  3. Run the official cPanel installation utility inside a screen session to ensure uninterrupted execution: 
    screen -S cpanel-install
curl -o latest -L https://securedownloads.cpanel.net/latest
sh latest
  4. Activate your wholesale cPanel license through LicenBase and run /usr/local/cpanel/cpkeyclt to verify activation.
