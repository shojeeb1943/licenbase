==================================================

This is an ecommerce + SaaS hybrid homepage.

The user journey should be extremely clear:

Homepage
→ Category
→ Product
→ License Type
→ Cart
→ Checkout
→ License Delivery

Prioritize:

1. Product discovery
2. Deals
3. Bundles
4. Clear pricing
5. Trust
6. Fast checkout
7. Business licensing
8. Support

Make CTAs visually consistent.

Primary CTA:
Blue filled button

Secondary CTA:
White/outlined button

Sale CTA:
Blue or emerald accent

Do not make every section look identical.

Use visual rhythm:
- White sections
- Light gray sections
- Dark navy bundle/enterprise sections
- White product cards
- Strong blue CTAs

- Consistent spacing system
- Consistent card radius

- Accessible contrast
- Realistic ecommerce UI patterns
- Polished hover states

- Random decorative shapes
- Excessive animations
- Excessive gradients
- Glassmorphism
- Neon colors

- Cheap marketplace aesthetics
