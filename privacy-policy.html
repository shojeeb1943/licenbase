1. Overview & Data Controller

This Privacy Policy applies to the collection, processing, transmission, and retention of personal and corporate data collected by LicenBase ("LicenBase", "we", "us", or "our") when you interact with licenbase.com, purchase software licenses, bind server IP credentials, or communicate with our engineering support teams.

LicenBase is the data controller responsible for your personal information. We are committed to transparency, GDPR compliance (EU Regulation 2016/679), CCPA compliance (California Consumer Privacy Act), and industry best practices for enterprise information security.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on your interactions with our marketplace, we may collect the following categories of data:

Account & Identity

Full name, company or organization name, business email address, billing address, phone number, and account authentication credentials.

Technical & Server Data

Public server IPv4/IPv6 addresses, server hostnames, virtualization environment type, hardware IDs, and operating system identifiers required for license activation.

Payment & Financial Data

Payment transaction reference IDs, partial credit card digits (last 4), billing records, and tax identifiers (VAT / EIN). Full payment card data is processed directly by PCI-DSS Level 1 payment processors.

Usage & Telemetry

IP address, browser type, referral URLs, device fingerprinting for fraud prevention, interaction logs, and support chat transcripts.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process your data strictly for legitimate commercial, contractual, and operational requirements, including:

  • License Fulfillment & Server Activation: Communicating with upstream licensing APIs (e.g. cPanel, LiteSpeed, CloudLinux, Microsoft) to bind and authenticate software licenses to your designated IP or hardware.
  • Billing & Invoicing: Generating compliant corporate invoices, processing renewal subscriptions, and maintaining accounting ledgers.
  • Fraud Prevention & License Integrity: Detecting unauthorized chargebacks, license piracy, abusive IP rotations, and suspicious checkout activity.
  • Customer & Technical Support: Responding to helpdesk tickets, license transfer requests, and live chat assistance.
  • Compliance with Legal & Vendor Obligations: Satisfying mandatory vendor audit requirements and tax compliance.

4. Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR & CCPA)

Under global data protection regulations, our processing of your personal information is justified under the following legal bases:

  • Performance of Contract: Processing necessary to fulfill your license order and maintain active digital provisioning.
  • Legitimate Interests: Securing our marketplace against fraud, safeguarding software vendor intellectual property, and improving infrastructure performance.
  • Legal Compliance: Maintaining fiscal records, tax reporting, and anti-money-laundering (AML) checks.
  • Consent: Sending voluntary product updates, newsletters, or marketing communications (which can be opted out at any time).

5. Third-Party Sharing & Upstream Vendor APIs

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties for advertising purposes. Information is shared only with vetted service providers and software manufacturers necessary to deliver your services:

  • Software Manufacturers & Registries: When you purchase an IP-bound license (e.g., cPanel, LiteSpeed, CloudLinux, Plesk, Virtualizor, Imunify360) or cloud seat (Microsoft 365), we transmit the required server IP, domain, or organization identifier to the vendor’s centralized licensing registry to validate and activate the license.
  • Payment Gateways: PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant processors (e.g., Stripe, PayPal, wire banks) process billing data securely over 256-bit SSL encryption.
  • Infrastructure & Email Gateways: Cloud hosting providers and transactional email providers (e.g., AWS, SendGrid) to dispatch license delivery emails.

6. Data Security & Encryption Standards

LicenBase implements enterprise-grade technical and organizational safeguards:

Security Standards & Practices
  • All data in transit is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
  • Digital license tokens, credentials, and sensitive server data are stored with AES-256 encryption at rest.
  • Role-based access controls (RBAC) restrict internal personnel access to customer license vaults.
  • Regular automated vulnerability assessments and automated threat detection mechanisms.

7. Data Retention Policy

We retain personal and business information for as long as your customer account remains active or as needed to provide license management services. In addition, financial transaction logs, IP activation histories, and corporate invoices are retained for a minimum of seven (7) years to comply with statutory accounting, taxation, and anti-fraud regulations.

8. Your Privacy & Data Rights

Depending on your regional jurisdiction (such as the EU, UK, or California), you possess statutory privacy rights regarding your data:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete corporate/billing information.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request deletion of your personal data, subject to statutory tax retention and active license validation constraints.
  • Right to Data Portability: Obtain an export of your account and licensing history in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Restrict or Object: Object to processing based on legitimate interests or direct marketing.

9. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

LicenBase uses essential cookies, performance cookies, and analytical tools to deliver a responsive browsing experience, maintain active sessions, remember your shopping preferences, and prevent malicious bot attacks. You can manage or disable non-essential cookies via your browser settings; however, disabling certain cookies may impact the checkout and account portal functionality.
