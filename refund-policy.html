Official Policy Directive

1. Strict No-Refund Policy After Purchase

ALL TRANSACTIONS CONDUCTED ON LICENBASE ARE FINAL. WE DO NOT OFFER CASH REFUNDS, PAYMENT REVERSALS, OR ORDER EXCHANGES ONCE A DIGITAL PRODUCT HAS BEEN PURCHASED AND DELIVERED.

LicenBase provides intangible, cryptographic, digitally provisioned software licenses (including retail license keys, volume license credentials, server IP activations, and cloud SaaS seats).

From the moment an order is confirmed and the license key, activation token, or server IP binding is transmitted to you via email or dashboard, the purchase is deemed consumed, fully executed, and non-refundable.

2. Why Digital Software Licenses Cannot Be Refunded

The non-refundable nature of our products is governed by the technical realities of digital software distribution and international ecommerce standards for digital goods:

Irrevocable Disclosure

Unlike physical goods, once a 25-character digital license key or activation credential is viewed or revealed, it cannot be physically returned or un-seen.

Upstream Vendor Commitment

When you purchase server licenses (such as cPanel, LiteSpeed, CloudLinux, Plesk), our automated API directly provisions and pays for the activation on the software vendor's central registry.

Anti-Piracy Protection

To prevent license exploitation, key duplication, and black-market harvesting, software manufacturers do not credit or cancel issued license batches.

Automated Fulfillment

Our fulfillment pipeline operates 24/7 in real time. Delivery occurs instantaneously upon payment clearance.

3. Explicit Waiver of Statutory Withdrawal Rights

In accordance with European Union Directive 2011/83/EU, UK Consumer Contracts Regulations, and applicable international consumer protection laws governing digital content:

Express Consent & Waiver: By checking the agreement box at checkout and completing your order, you expressly request and consent to the immediate performance of this contract (instant electronic provisioning of digital software goods), and you acknowledge that you lose your statutory 14-day right of withdrawal or cooling-off period once the digital download, key delivery, or server activation has begun.

4. Narrow Pre-Delivery Cancellation Exception

The only instance in which an order may be cancelled and refunded is if:

  • Your order has entered a manual review queue or fraud verification hold; AND
  • The digital license key has NOT yet been generated, revealed, dispatched to your email, or bound to your designated server IP address.

If you realize an error in your order (e.g. incorrect license tier or mistyped IP) before provisioning has occurred, you must immediately contact our emergency support at support@licenbase.com with your order number. Once delivery is processed, no cancellation is possible.

5. Defective or Invalid License Verification & Replacement

LicenBase guarantees 100% genuine, authentic software licenses sourced exclusively through legitimate channels. In the rare event that an issued license key is reported as non-functional or invalid:

Replacement Protocol (72-Hour Reporting Window):

  1. Report within 72 hours: You must submit a support ticket to support@licenbase.com within 72 hours of purchase.
  2. Provide Diagnostic Proof: Submit an unedited, full-screen screenshot displaying the exact error message, system date/time, and software version, or copy/paste the server terminal activation output (for Linux control panel licenses).
  3. Vendor Verification: Our engineering team verifies the key status against the upstream software vendor's activation servers (Microsoft KMS/Retail registry, cPanel verify portal, LiteSpeed license database).
  4. Resolution: If the key is verified defective or unactivated by the upstream vendor, LicenBase will issue a replacement license key or store credit within 24 business hours. Cash refunds are not issued.

* Note: Issues arising from incompatible customer hardware, unsupported operating system versions, unauthorized third-party modifications, corrupted operating systems, or failure to meet manufacturer minimum requirements do not constitute a defective key.

6. Recurring Subscriptions & Cancellation Terms

For monthly, quarterly, or annual recurring software licenses (such as cPanel VPS licenses, CloudLinux, LiteSpeed, or Microsoft 365 cloud subscriptions):

  • Cancel Anytime: You may cancel your subscription renewal at any time directly through your LicenBase Account Dashboard or by submitting a cancellation ticket prior to the renewal billing date.
  • Active Period Access: When you cancel a subscription, your license remains active through the end of your current paid billing period.
  • No Prorated Refunds: We do not issue partial, prorated, or retrospective refunds for unused portions of an active subscription month or year.

7. Chargebacks, Payment Disputes & Anti-Fraud Measures

Initiating an unauthorized chargeback, credit card dispute, or PayPal claim for a digitally delivered and valid software license violates our Terms of Service and constitutes commercial fraud.

In the event of an unjustified chargeback:

  • All software licenses associated with your account will be immediately and permanently revoked on the vendor's central servers.
  • Your server IP address, domain, company details, and billing identities will be blacklisted across our partner licensing networks.
  • Full transaction telemetry, delivery timestamps, IP binding records, and cryptographically verified access logs will be submitted to financial institutions and debt collection agencies.
